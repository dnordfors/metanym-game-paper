# Author bio — ICLR 2027 (OpenReview profile / post-decision use; not reviewer-visible — ICLR is double-blind)

## Full (~130 words)

David Nordfors is an independent researcher in Los Altos, California. His work centers on the
evaluation of AI systems where no answer key exists — including the Metanym Game, a
self-contained benchmark in which language models generate all test material and grade one
another, with a single SVD over the ratings matrix recovering each model's generation and
judging competence — and, currently, embedding spaces read out of a frozen LLM's hidden states
that disentangle a text's narrative structure from its topic. From 2019 to 2026 he was
Principal Research Scientist in the Office of the CEO at BOLD, building machine-learning
systems for labor-market text at scale. He holds a Ph.D. in quantum physics from Uppsala
University, a pending patent family on interacting LLM agents, and earlier spent two decades
founding and leading research institutions, including Stanford's innovation-journalism program
and — with Vint Cerf — the i4j "Innovation for Jobs" initiative, co-authoring two books with
Cerf.
