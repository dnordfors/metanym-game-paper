### Archetypal context 1: Selective Boundary

#### Factually correct (per parallel context)

- PC 1 (Cell Membrane): The description of selective membrane permeability, transport proteins discriminating among molecules, and the dual risk of toxin entry versus nutrient starvation is biologically accurate and consistent with standard cell biology. The Form (b) rewrite correctly invokes the calibration problem. This is at least as accurate as the Reference's bacterial chemotaxis context. Rating: 7

- PC 2 (National Border): The framing of visitors as productive, hostile actors as threats, and border officers as gatekeepers is a standard and defensible political-science framing. The Form (b) correctly identifies both undersecurity and oversecurity as failure modes. No factual claims are made that are wrong. Rating: 7

- PC 3 (Computer Network): Firewalls, legitimate vs. malicious packets, and the permissive/restrictive tradeoff are all standard cybersecurity concepts accurately represented. Form (b) correctly uses "intrusions" and "block valid traffic." Rating: 7

- PC 4 (Publishing House): The analogy of editorial selection as a boundary against fraud and unusable manuscripts is coherent and defensible. "Solvency" as the viability stake is reasonable for a commercial publisher. No factually incorrect claims. Rating: 7

- PC 5 (Personal Relationship): The psychological claim that excessive disclosure risks exploitation, while excessive guardedness prevents intimacy, is well-grounded in attachment and boundary literature. Form (b) correctly frames boundary-setting as calibration, not withdrawal. Rating: 7

#### Beauty
The template is clean and the central tension—calibrated exchange rather than elimination—is elegantly expressed. The closing line, "tuning the boundary so that exchange remains compatible with viability," is a satisfying formulation. The Form (b) rewrites are consistently polished, especially for the personal relationship domain where "calibrated openness" feels earned. Against the Reference, which achieves comparable elegance over a considerably longer template, this reads as competent but not distinctively more beautiful given its brevity. Rating: 7

#### Intelligence
The insight that every boundary faces the same dual failure mode (too permissive vs. too restrictive) and that regulation is a tuning problem rather than an on/off switch is genuine and well articulated. However, the cell membrane = border = firewall comparison is among the most commonly cited cross-domain analogies in science writing, reducing the surprise value. The inclusion of "personal relationship" and "publishing house" is the more novel move. Comparable to the Reference overall. Rating: 7

#### Domains far apart / metanyms not synonymous
The five domains—cell biology, geopolitics, computing, publishing, and interpersonal psychology—are meaningfully distinct. Key metanyms diverge well: GATEKEEPER runs from transport protein to border officer to security rule to editor to judgment; VIABILITY runs from survival to sovereignty to reliability to solvency to mutual well-being. A minor weakness is that BOUNDARY maps literally to "boundary" in the personal relationship column, lacking a domain-specific term. Rating: 7

#### Impressive length
The template contains five sentences and ten slots. This is substantially shorter than the Reference template (nine sentences, ten slots). The template captures the core idea efficiently but sacrifices depth: it does not address temporal dynamics, collective signaling, or the conditions under which boundary failure cascades—nuances the Reference template includes. Rating: 5

Slightly above the Reference in elegance of formulation, though constrained by brevity. Rating: 8

### Archetypal context 3: Gradient-Driven Allocation

#### Factually correct (per parallel context)

- PC 1 (Gas Diffusion): Molecules diffuse down concentration gradients, partitions impede diffusion, compressors drive gas against the gradient at the cost of mechanical work. Entirely accurate. Form (b) is correctly expressed. Rating: 8

- PC 2 (Labor Market): Form (a) states "a worker tends to move from a region of high wage opportunity toward a region of low wage opportunity." This is factually backwards: workers migrate toward higher wages, not away from them. Workers move from regions of low wage opportunity to high wage opportunity. Form (b) correctly says "Workers tend to relocate toward places offering better wages," directly contradicting Form (a). This is a significant factual error in Form (a). Rating: 3

- PC 3 (Water Basin): Water flows from high hydraulic head to low hydraulic head, dams block flow, pump stations lift water against gravity consuming electricity. All accurate and precisely expressed. Rating: 8

- PC 4 (Information Ecosystem): Form (a) states a message moves "from a region of high audience attention toward a region of low audience attention," which is defensible as a diffusion model (content spreads from already-saturated audiences to less-reached ones). Form (b) supports this reading. The mapping is somewhat strained—"audience attention" is a proxy for exposure density rather than demand—but not flatly wrong. Rating: 6

- PC 5 (Urban Housing): Form (a) states households move "from a region of high housing affordability toward a region of low housing affordability." Empirically, households move *toward* more affordable locations when priced out; this is again backwards. Form (b) correctly says "Households tend to seek more affordable housing locations." A systematic factual error parallel to the labor market case. Rating: 4

#### Beauty
The three-part decomposition—spontaneous equalization, barriers, and energy-consuming pumps—is elegant and tight. The gas diffusion and water basin contexts read beautifully. However, the factual inversions in labor and housing break the analogy's credibility for readers who know those domains, and this undermines the archetype's aesthetic coherence. Below the Reference. Rating: 6

#### Intelligence
The abstraction of diffusion + barrier + pump as a universal allocation mechanism is genuinely insightful and the three-way contest is a sharp organizing principle. However, the mis-mapping of CONCENTRATION in labor and housing markets (treating wage opportunity as something workers move away from rather than toward) reveals incomplete thinking in the template's application. The template concept is more intelligent than its execution. Rating: 6

#### Domains far apart / metanyms not synonymous
Gas diffusion, labor markets, hydrology, information ecosystems, and urban housing are substantively distinct domains spanning physics, economics, engineering, media studies, and urban planning. The metanyms are reasonably differentiated: BARRIER maps to partition, licensing barrier, dam, paywall, zoning restriction; PUMP maps to compressor, recruitment subsidy, pump station, promotion campaign, housing subsidy. Good diversity. Rating: 7

#### Impressive length
Five sentences, eight slots—the fewest slots of any archetype in the submission. The template is the most minimal of the set and does not address equilibrium conditions, feedback between pump and gradient, or systemic consequences of prolonged barrier maintenance. Well below the Reference in template length. Rating: 4

### Archetypal context 4: Feedback-Controlled Pursuit

#### Factually correct (per parallel context)

- PC 1 (Thermostat): The closed-loop description—thermometer measures error, thermostat adjusts heater, weak response → discomfort, strong/delayed response → cycling—is textbook control theory. Accurate. Rating: 7

- PC 2 (Blood Glucose Regulation): Pancreatic glucose sensing, insulin secretion as actuator, hyperglycemia as the under-response failure, and glucose swings as the over-response failure are all accurate. The simplification of treating the pancreas as both sensor and controller is a permissible abstraction. Rating: 7

- PC 3 (Cruise Control): Speed sensor, throttle as actuator, speed shortfall vs. hunting—all accurate descriptions of automotive cruise control. Rating: 7

- PC 4 (Monetary Policy): Central bank targeting an inflation rate via policy rate adjustments, with persistent inflation as the under-response failure and boom-bust as the over-response failure, is an accurate characterization of modern monetary policy frameworks. Rating: 7

- PC 5 (Project Management): Progress reports as sensors, resource allocation as actuator, delay as under-response failure, and schedule churn as over-response failure are all reasonable and accurate characterizations of project control practice. Rating: 7

#### Beauty
The template is clean and follows a satisfying logical arc. The "too weak / too strong or delayed" pairing is economical and correct. Form (b) rewrites are uniformly polished and consistently capture the calibration theme. However, feedback control is the most canonical system archetype in engineering and systems thinking—there is nothing surprising about the parallel contexts or the template formulation. Comparable to the Reference but not distinctive. Rating: 7

#### Intelligence
The feedback control loop is one of the most exhaustively analyzed archetypes in science and engineering. The template accurately captures the essential structure, and the application to monetary policy and project management is apt. But the archetype itself lacks novelty—PID control as metaphor has been applied to exactly these domains many times before. The submission does not add a new angle or unexpected insight. Slightly below the Reference in intellectual ambition. Rating: 6

#### Domains far apart / metanyms not synonymous
The five domains—engineering (thermostat), physiology, automotive systems, macroeconomics, and management—are reasonably spread. However, thermostat and cruise control are both canonical engineering control systems and occupy very similar conceptual territory. ACTUATOR maps to heater, insulin secretion, throttle, policy rate, resource allocation—reasonable variation. OSCILLATION maps to temperature cycling, glucose swing, speed hunting, boom-bust cycle, schedule churn—good variation. The thermostat/cruise control overlap slightly weakens this criterion. Rating: 6

#### Impressive length
Five sentences, ten slots. The template does not describe how the error signal is weighted (proportional vs. integral vs. derivative), how multiple feedback loops interact, conditions under which the sensor itself can fail, or how targets are set. These are all real features of the archetype. Shorter and less detailed than the Reference. Rating: 5

### Archetypal context 5: Layered Translation

#### Factually correct (per parallel context)

- PC 1 (Genetic Expression): The description of tRNA reading codons and delivering amino acids is accurate. However, Form (a) states the gene and ribosome "use different nucleotide sequences" as the CODE mismatch, which is awkward—the actual code gap is between nucleotide triplets (codons) and amino acids, not between two types of nucleotide sequence. The justification correctly notes mRNA as intermediary. Slightly misleading but not egregiously wrong. Rating: 6

- PC 2 (Computer Compilation): Source program → intermediate representation → machine code, compiler as translator, syntax vs. computational intent as form vs. meaning—all precisely accurate and technically well-expressed. One of the strongest factual instantiations in the whole submission. Rating: 8

- PC 3 (Legal Interpretation): Statute → legal construction → ruling, with the form/purpose distinction as the fidelity criterion, is an accurate and sophisticated description of statutory interpretation theory. The two failure modes (literalism losing purpose; purposivism producing unenforceable rulings) are real jurisprudential categories. Rating: 8

- PC 4 (International Diplomacy): Policy statement → negotiated formulation → agreement, with the two failure modes (formulaic language obscuring intent; intent expressed in unacceptable terms), is an accurate characterization of diplomatic translation. Rating: 7

- PC 5 (Music Performance): Score → interpretive conception → sounding phrase, with literal adherence vs. expressive overreach as failure modes, is a defensible and accurate characterization of performance practice theory. "That the audience can use" is an unusual phrasing but the underlying idea is correct. Rating: 7

#### Beauty
The final sentence of the template—"Failures of translation arise when the translator preserves surface form but loses meaning, or preserves intended meaning but produces unusable form"—is the most beautiful single sentence in the entire Target submission. The two-failure-mode structure is philosophically crisp and the music performance domain is handled with genuine literary quality in Form (b). This is clearly above the Reference in beauty. Rating: 8

#### Intelligence
The form/meaning distinction as the universal tension in translation is a philosophically sophisticated organizing principle. Applying it simultaneously to molecular biology (codon → amino acid), computer science (source → machine code), jurisprudence (statute → ruling), diplomacy (policy → agreement), and musical performance (notation → sound) is intellectually ambitious and largely successful. The two failure modes (surface-form preservation that loses meaning; meaning preservation that produces unusable form) are analytically precise and non-obvious. This is the most intelligent archetype in the submission, above the Reference. Rating: 8

#### Domains far apart / metanyms not synonymous
The five domains—molecular biology, computer science, law, international relations, and music—are among the most diverse in the submission. Key metanyms diverge sharply: TRANSLATOR maps to tRNA, compiler, judge, diplomat, performer; REPRESENTATION maps to anticodon pairing, intermediate representation, legal construction, negotiated formulation, interpretive conception; OUTPUT maps to amino acid sequence, machine code, ruling, agreement, sounding phrase. Excellent metanym diversity. Rating: 8

#### Impressive length
Four sentences, eleven slots. This is the shortest template in terms of sentence count, though it is dense. The template does not address chain-of-translation failures, fidelity degradation over multiple translation steps, or how the translator's own perspective inflects the output. Below the Reference in depth of elaboration. Rating: 5

### Structural diversity across the submitted set

The five archetypes—Selective Boundary, Distributed Consensus, Gradient-Driven Allocation, Feedback-Controlled Pursuit, and Layered Translation—have genuinely distinct system structures. Selective Boundary is about filter calibration; Distributed Consensus is about epistemic aggregation under adversarial noise; Gradient-Driven Allocation is about thermodynamic-style equalization and its counterforces; Feedback-Controlled Pursuit is about closed-loop regulation; and Layered Translation is about code-crossing fidelity. There is some structural proximity between Selective Boundary and Feedback-Controlled Pursuit (both involve regulatory mechanisms that can err in two directions), and between Gradient-Driven Allocation and Selective Boundary (both involve barriers to flow). The Reference set—gradient navigation, containment breach cascade, competitive exclusion, debt accumulation, and scaffold assembly—presents structures that feel more mutually orthogonal, with the added dimension of a recursive archetype. The Target set is solid and clearly diverse, roughly at the same level as the Reference but without the recursive elaboration. Rating: 7
