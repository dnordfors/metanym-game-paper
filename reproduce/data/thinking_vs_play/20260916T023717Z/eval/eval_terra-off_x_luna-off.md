## Target Submission

### Archetypal context 1: Thresholded Cascade

#### Factually correct (per parallel context)
- PC 1 (Neuronal firing): Relative to the Reference, this is more factually disciplined and idiomatic. Threshold crossing, voltage-gated channel propagation, inhibition, refractory dynamics, and stochastic channel behavior are all broadly correct, though “synaptic connectivity” is less central to propagation along a single neuron than to network-level firing. Rating: 8
- PC 2 (Wildfire spread): This is a strong and accurate mapping: ignition, fuel continuity, moisture, smoldering delay, radiant-heating feedback, fuel loads, and wind all plausibly govern thresholded fire spread. It is cleaner than several Reference Form (a) substitutions. Rating: 8
- PC 3 (Financial panic): The account correctly captures liquidity runs, confidence feedback, interbank contagion, settlement delays, and deposit insurance. It somewhat conflates a withdrawal-driven liquidity crisis with a solvency threshold, but Form (b) appropriately qualifies this as a liquidity or solvency buffer. Rating: 8
- PC 4 (Epidemic transmission): The epidemiological account is broadly correct, especially its emphasis on contact structure, immunity, incubation, susceptible pools, reporting noise, and self-sustaining transmission. “Transmission threshold” is somewhat generic rather than explicitly framed as effective reproduction number above one, but the prose conveys the idea accurately. Rating: 8
- PC 5 (Nuclear chain reaction): This is accurate at a high level: criticality, neutron multiplication, generation time, leakage, and stochasticity are appropriately connected. “Neutron absorption exceeds the criticality threshold” is imprecise, since criticality depends on effective multiplication factor rather than absorption alone, but Form (b) substantially repairs the formulation. Rating: 8

#### Beauty
The template is compact, coherent, and rhetorically controlled. Its progression from threshold crossing through cascade dynamics, feedback, state transition, and observability gives it a satisfying conceptual arc. It is more elegant and less mechanically repetitive than much of the Reference. Rating: 8

#### Intelligence
This archetype identifies a genuinely general systems structure rather than merely sharing vocabulary across domains. Coupling, delay, damping, feedback, hidden reservoirs, and uncertainty about timing are meaningful cross-domain abstractions. The selection of domains demonstrates sophisticated systems thinking. Rating: 9

#### Domains far apart / metanyms not synonymous
The domains span neurophysiology, ecology, banking, epidemiology, and nuclear physics, which are substantially more diverse than many Reference tables. The metanyms are structurally parallel without being synonyms, especially potential/reserve, damping, feedback, and observer. Rating: 9

#### Impressive length
The template is moderately long and information-dense, but it is notably shorter than the Reference templates. Its concision is a stylistic strength, yet on the specific length criterion it is somewhat below the calibration anchor. Rating: 6

### Archetypal context 2: Boundary Exchange

#### Factually correct (per parallel context)
- PC 1 (Cell membrane): The cellular account is broadly accurate: selective permeability, nutrient uptake, metabolite export, toxin exclusion, channels, receptors, osmotic stress, and homeostasis all fit. “Buffering capacity” is a somewhat broad substitute for osmotic tolerance, but the overall account is sound. Rating: 8
- PC 2 (City wall and gate): This is historically and conceptually plausible. Cities regulate supplies, waste, access, and defense through walls, gates, officials, and reserves. The claim that excessive openness necessarily loses civic order is generalized, but it works as an archetypal statement rather than a literal universal. Rating: 8
- PC 3 (Software API): The mapping is mostly accurate: APIs accept requests, return responses, reject malformed inputs, and use authorization and throughput controls. “Changes contract or ceases to persist” is somewhat overstated for overload, but it remains a plausible failure-or-adaptation outcome. Rating: 8
- PC 4 (Diplomatic border): The account accurately captures border regulation, trade, enforcement, sovereignty, and absorption limits. “Removes treaties” in Form (a) is awkward and not literally analogous to output transport, though Form (b) improves it by describing agreements enacted through the border. Rating: 7
- PC 5 (Museum collection): This is imaginative and mostly coherent, with accession, provenance, storage, curatorial selection, and forgery exclusion fitting well. However, exhibitions are not simply “removed” from a collection, and indiscriminate acquisition does not itself destroy provenance; these are conceptual stretches. Rating: 7

#### Beauty
The template has a clear equilibrium structure and a pleasing tension between deprivation through closure and incoherence through openness. Its final emphasis on flows, constraints, and maintenance costs gives it analytical unity. It is at least as polished as the Reference. Rating: 8

#### Intelligence
The archetype successfully generalizes selective exchange and boundary maintenance across biological, civic, technical, political, and cultural systems. It recognizes that persistence depends not on maximal closure but on regulated permeability, which is a strong systems insight. Rating: 8

#### Domains far apart / metanyms not synonymous
The domains are notably varied, ranging from membranes and APIs to cities, states, and museums. The museum mapping is more metaphorically stretched than the others, but that stretch also demonstrates non-synonymous metanyms and broad domain reach. Rating: 8

#### Impressive length
The template is coherent and moderately developed, but shorter than the Reference’s typical templates and contains fewer interacting roles and contingencies. Rating: 6

### Archetypal context 3: Distributed Coordination

#### Factually correct (per parallel context)
- PC 1 (Ant colony): This is a strong account of decentralized ant foraging. Queens do not centrally direct foraging, pheromone reinforcement supports convergence, and stale or misleading trails can produce inefficient behavior. “Chemical avoidance” is plausible but less universally central than pheromone signaling. Rating: 8
- PC 2 (Traffic network): The traffic account is broadly accurate, including local congestion cues, decentralized lane choices, braking waves, gridlock, route memory, and enforcement. The Form (a) phrase “changes its lane change” is grammatically defective, but Form (b) is clear and factually sound. Rating: 8
- PC 3 (Open-source project): This is accurate and well grounded: issue reports, contribution protocols, commits, version history, review, merge conflicts, and decentralized maintenance all fit. “Sanction” through code review is metaphorical but defensible as a quality-control mechanism. Rating: 8
- PC 4 (Orchestra): The account is mostly accurate, though an orchestra ordinarily does have a conductor with substantial coordinating authority, making it a weaker example of coordination without a central commander. Form (b) acknowledges that the conductor cannot monitor every detail, but the domain only partially instantiates the stated premise. Rating: 7
- PC 5 (Immune system): This is a strong decentralized-systems mapping. Local antigen recognition, cell-cell signaling, memory, regulatory suppression, coordinated activation, and autoimmune failure are all appropriate. “Strategically distorted” signals are less literal in immunology, but the core claims are sound. Rating: 8

#### Beauty
The template is lucid and balanced, moving from local observation and rules to emergent coordination, failure propagation, memory, sanction, and global performance. It has a strong explanatory rhythm and is more graceful than the Reference’s more repetitive formulations. Rating: 8

#### Intelligence
This is one of the strongest archetypes in the submission. It captures the central problem of distributed systems: incomplete local information, emergent global order, distorted signals, institutional memory, and corrective mechanisms. The inclusion of both coordination and runaway imitation adds real conceptual depth. Rating: 9

#### Domains far apart / metanyms not synonymous
Ant colonies, traffic, open-source development, orchestras, and immune systems are highly distinct domains. The orchestra is structurally less pure because of the conductor, but the overall table remains diverse and the metanyms are clearly non-synonymous. Rating: 9

#### Impressive length
The template is substantial enough to articulate several linked mechanisms, but it remains shorter than the Reference templates. Rating: 6

### Archetypal context 4: Layered Transformation

#### Factually correct (per parallel context)
- PC 1 (Digestion): The broad account of staged digestion, enzymatic selectivity, chyme, hormonal regulation, and unabsorbed material is accurate. However, an organism does not literally “leave as a structured nutrient,” and enzymes generally break or modify bonds rather than preserve selected bonds in the stated way. Rating: 7
- PC 2 (Compiler pipeline): This is a strong mapping of source code, compiler passes, intermediate representations, diagnostics, build configuration, and error propagation. The table’s use of “type checker” as the filter for every compiler pass is too narrow, but Form (b) corrects this by referring to checks and rewrites more generally. Rating: 8
- PC 3 (Legal adjudication): The legal mapping is coherent: hearings and procedural stages filter evidence, records carry earlier rulings forward, and excluded arguments mark unusable material. “Institutional authority determines the rate” is somewhat abstract, but the overall account is accurate. Rating: 8
- PC 4 (Film production): This is broadly accurate and idiomatic. Film production does proceed through phases, editorial decisions preserve or reshape dramatic material, rough cuts carry earlier decisions forward, and budget and schedule constrain outcomes. Rating: 8
- PC 5 (Geological metamorphism): The geological account is broadly correct regarding protoliths, pressure-temperature conditions, mineral reactions, inherited constraints, heat, stress, and expelled volatiles. “Ordered metamorphic facies” is somewhat misleading because facies are assemblage conditions rather than necessarily sequential stages, but Form (b) improves the framing. Rating: 7

#### Beauty
The template has a satisfying processual structure and uses the idea of reconstructing hidden stages to unify otherwise disparate transformations. It is elegant, though some wording—especially “structured output” and “what the process cannot incorporate”—becomes slightly mechanical across domains. Rating: 8

#### Intelligence
The archetype insightfully connects pipelines in which early constraints propagate, errors may be amplified or corrected, and final quality requires retrospective reconstruction. It is conceptually strong, though less surprising than Thresholded Cascade or Distributed Coordination. Rating: 8

#### Domains far apart / metanyms not synonymous
Digestion, compilation, adjudication, filmmaking, and metamorphism are very far apart. The mappings are generally structural rather than lexical, and the table makes good use of non-synonymous counterparts. Rating: 9

#### Impressive length
This is among the more developed Target templates, with multiple stages, filters, intermediates, controls, waste, and retrospective evaluation. Still, it is shorter than the Reference’s longest templates but close enough in conceptual density to meet the anchor. Rating: 7

### Archetypal context 5: Renewal and Succession

#### Factually correct (per parallel context)
- PC 1 (Forest succession): The ecological account is broadly plausible: pioneer vegetation alters light conditions, litter and shading affect recruitment, shade-tolerant species can replace pioneers, and fire and seed banks shape succession. However, fire is not always the mediating transition in succession, and ash is often more enabling than inhibiting depending on context. Rating: 8
- PC 2 (Corporate succession): The corporate analogy is coherent and plausible: organizational complexity can raise coordination costs, bankruptcy can redistribute customers, talent, and assets, and inherited debt can constrain successors. It is less universally causal than the ecological case but remains factually defensible. Rating: 8
- PC 3 (Urban redevelopment): The account is plausible but somewhat strained. Infrastructure decay can raise vacancy and redevelopment pressure, demolition can expose or alter inherited urban structure, and rubble can obstruct or be reused. Yet “reduces developable space” is not generally true; decay may instead create redevelopment opportunity. Rating: 7
- PC 4 (Software-platform succession): This is accurate and strong. Technical debt, maintenance burden, contributor barriers, deprecation, migration, data formats, and compatibility layers are all meaningful mechanisms of platform succession. Rating: 8
- PC 5 (Ecological gut succession): The account is broadly accurate about microbial succession, substrate depletion, metabolite effects, antibiotic disturbance, recolonization, and resistance. “Exposes microbial DNA” is a somewhat speculative or overly generalized mechanism, but the overall ecological framing is sound. Rating: 8

#### Beauty
The template has a compelling historical sensibility: systems transform their own niches, leave residues, and make succession neither pure replacement nor total reset. That final contrast is particularly elegant and gives the archetype more narrative force than the Reference average. Rating: 8

#### Intelligence
This is a sophisticated ecological and institutional pattern. It captures niche construction, path dependence, disturbance, legacy effects, recruitment, and succession in a way that transfers effectively across domains. Some mappings are more metaphorical than causal, but the underlying abstraction is strong. Rating: 9

#### Domains far apart / metanyms not synonymous
The domains span forest ecology, firms, urban planning, software platforms, and gut microbiology. They are diverse, though forest and gut succession share ecological lineage and therefore are somewhat closer than the strongest Target tables. Rating: 8

#### Impressive length
The template is moderately long and contains several interacting mechanisms—self-altered conditions, accumulation, stress, disturbance, legacy, recruitment, and phase observation. It remains shorter than the Reference benchmark but is reasonably substantial. Rating: 7

### Structural diversity across the submitted set
Relative to the Reference, the five Target archetypes exhibit stronger structural diversity. They cover nonlinear threshold cascades, selective boundary exchange, decentralized coordination, staged transformation pipelines, and succession through niche modification and legacy effects. These are genuinely different system architectures rather than variants of a single competition, containment, or accumulation pattern. Rating: 9
