### Archetypal context 1: Thresholded Cascade

#### Factually correct (per parallel context)
- PC 1 (Neuronal firing): Compared with the Reference’s 7-level factual reliability, this is stronger: threshold crossing, ion-channel propagation, refractory behavior, inhibition, and stochastic channel variation are all sound. Synaptic connectivity is more relevant to recurrent circuit dynamics than to propagation along one neuron, but the rewrite handles that nuance reasonably. Rating: 8
- PC 2 (Wildfire spread): This is notably more factually coherent than the Reference average. Fuel continuity, moisture, smoldering delay, radiant-heating feedback, and uncertain fuel loads and winds all plausibly determine whether ignition becomes spread. Rating: 9
- PC 3 (Financial panic): The account accurately captures bank-run feedback, interbank contagion, settlement delays, insurance, and opacity. It somewhat conflates liquidity failure with insolvency—withdrawals usually first test liquidity rather than solvency—but Form (b) substantially corrects this. Rating: 8
- PC 4 (Epidemic transmission): The epidemiological account is sound in relating transmission replacement, contact structure, immunity, incubation, susceptible pools, and reporting noise to outbreak growth. Moving toward endemicity is not inevitable after a threshold crossing, but it is a plausible possible regime. Rating: 8
- PC 5 (Nuclear chain reaction): The rewrite accurately describes criticality as neutron reproduction sufficient to sustain fission. Form (a) is less precise in treating neutron absorption itself as the trigger for criticality, since criticality depends on the total neutron economy, but this remains materially cleaner than many Reference factual substitutions. Rating: 8

#### Beauty
Compared with the Reference, this template is more compact, rhythmically controlled, and conceptually unified. The threshold/cascade vocabulary produces a lucid progression from trigger through feedback to observability without excessive ornamental terminology. Rating: 8

#### Intelligence
The archetype identifies a genuinely deep common structure: nonlinear threshold behavior under coupling, delay, damping, feedback, hidden state, and noise. Its distinction between predicting direction and predicting timing is particularly insightful and stronger than a merely superficial cascade analogy. Rating: 8

#### Domains far apart / metanyms not synonymous
The domains range from cellular electrophysiology and wildfire ecology to banking, epidemiology, and reactor physics. The metanyms are role-equivalent rather than synonymous, and the physical, social, biological, and engineered systems are farther apart than the Reference’s typical domain spread. Rating: 9

#### Impressive length
The template is concise and complete, but it is materially shorter than the Reference’s generally more extended archetypal templates. It covers the necessary mechanism well without reaching the Reference’s level of elaborated length. Rating: 6

### Archetypal context 2: Boundary Exchange

#### Factually correct (per parallel context)
- PC 1 (Cell membrane): This is largely accurate and clearer than the Reference’s average biological instantiation. Selective permeability, nutrient uptake, metabolite export, toxin exclusion, osmotic stress, and homeostatic failure are well stated, though receptors usually regulate exchange indirectly rather than functioning as transporters themselves. Rating: 8
- PC 2 (City wall and gate): The city analogy is historically and materially plausible: gates regulate provisioning and security, siege tests reserves, and boundary maintenance has real costs. Its claims are generalized but more coherent than the Reference’s weaker literal substitutions. Rating: 8
- PC 3 (Software API): The API account correctly frames interfaces as governing requests, responses, authorization, malformed data, and throughput limits. Calling low access scope “deprivation” is metaphorical rather than technical, but the Form (b) prose makes the operational meaning clear. Rating: 8
- PC 4 (Diplomatic border): This context is somewhat weaker. Borders do not literally “remove treaties,” and political change following refugee inflow is contingent rather than a direct boundary-capacity consequence. The rewrite is sensible, but the original substitution is only around the Reference benchmark. Rating: 7
- PC 5 (Museum collection): The discussion of acquisition, circulation, forgery exclusion, storage limits, and curatorial control is plausible. However, accession policy is not exactly a boundary that “removes exhibitions,” and indiscriminate acquisition does not automatically destroy provenance. Rating: 7

#### Beauty
This is an elegant and readable formulation of the permeability-versus-coherence trade-off. Compared with the Reference, the language is less repetitive and the final emphasis on flows, constraints, and maintenance costs gives the template an appealing analytic closure. Rating: 8

#### Intelligence
The template intelligently treats boundaries not as static walls but as costly, regulated systems of selective exchange. The paired failure modes—starvation under closure and loss of coherence under openness—generalize effectively across the selected domains. Rating: 8

#### Domains far apart / metanyms not synonymous
Cell biology, urban history, software architecture, international relations, and museum studies are substantially separated domains. The role mappings are generally non-synonymous and preserve the selective-boundary structure rather than simply relabeling a single concept. Rating: 9

#### Impressive length
The template has enough detail to articulate inputs, outputs, threats, regulation, capacity, and identity, but it remains shorter and less developed than the Reference’s 7-level standard for template length. Rating: 6

### Archetypal context 3: Distributed Coordination

#### Factually correct (per parallel context)
- PC 1 (Ant colony): This is a strong and accurate account of decentralized ant foraging. Pheromone-mediated local rules, reinforcement, stale or misleading trails, and avoidance chemistry are plausible mechanisms, although “strategic distortion” is more applicable to human systems than ants. Rating: 8
- PC 2 (Traffic network): The rewrite correctly explains local congestion cues, lane selection, braking waves, enforcement, and gridlock. Form (a) is awkward in saying a driver “changes its lane change,” and route history and fines only imperfectly fill the roles of memory and sanction. Rating: 7
- PC 3 (Open-source project): This is particularly strong relative to the Reference. Issue reports, contribution protocols, repository state, merge conflicts, version history, and code review all map naturally and accurately onto decentralized coordination. Rating: 9
- PC 4 (Orchestra): The account correctly emphasizes local listening, score-following, rehearsal, corrective feedback, and rhythmic drift. A conductor does possess substantial coordinating authority, but the more limited claim that no conductor has complete information remains true. Rating: 8
- PC 5 (Immune system): The immune-system version is broadly accurate: immune cells act locally, regulatory mechanisms suppress excessive responses, memory records prior encounters, and coordination can fail through autoimmunity. The analogy to “runaway imitation” is somewhat figurative but does not compromise the account. Rating: 8

#### Beauty
Compared with the Reference, the template has a notably clean cadence: local signal, local rule, local action, emergent coordination, and systemic misalignment. The prose is economical while still producing a coherent systems narrative. Rating: 8

#### Intelligence
This is among the Target’s most intelligent archetypes. It captures the central problem of distributed systems—global performance emerging from local, incomplete, incentive-sensitive information—and adds the important stabilizing roles of memory and sanction. Rating: 9

#### Domains far apart / metanyms not synonymous
The selected domains span insect colonies, transportation, collaborative software, music performance, and immunology. They are all coordination systems, as required, but their material substrates, incentives, and failure modes are meaningfully distinct. Rating: 8

#### Impressive length
The template is well structured and contains all core elements, but its five compact sentences are less extensive than the Reference’s longer archetypal formulations. Rating: 6

### Archetypal context 4: Layered Transformation

#### Factually correct (per parallel context)
- PC 1 (Digestion): The Form (b) account of digestion is sound, particularly regarding sequential compartments, enzymatic specificity, chyme, hormonal regulation, and waste. Form (a) is less exact in saying that an organism enters digestion as a meal and in implying each compartment applies a single enzyme, placing it around the Reference baseline overall. Rating: 7
- PC 2 (Compiler pipeline): This accurately captures source-to-executable transformation, intermediate representations, sequential passes, diagnostics, and configuration-dependent compilation. Not every compiler pass is a type-checking pass, but the idiomatic rewrite corrects the over-specificity effectively. Rating: 8
- PC 3 (Legal adjudication): This is a strong legal-process mapping. Evidentiary standards, procedural constraints, accumulating records, exclusion of material, review of earlier stages, and legitimacy are all suitably and carefully described. Rating: 8
- PC 4 (Film production): The rewrite correctly describes preparation, shooting, editing, and finishing as successive stages with inherited constraints. Form (a) overstates the role of an editorial cut in every production phase, but the overall account remains plausible and clearer than much of the Reference. Rating: 7
- PC 5 (Geological metamorphism): The description correctly invokes pressure-temperature conditions, mineral reactions, inherited precursors, tectonic stress, and volatile loss. Still, metamorphic facies are not always a simple ordered pipeline, and “error” is an imperfect geological characterization, keeping this near the Reference benchmark. Rating: 7

#### Beauty
This archetype has a strong narrative shape: input, selective stages, carried intermediates, energy and control, waste, and retrospective diagnosis. Compared with the Reference, it is unusually polished and accessible despite connecting technical and humanistic domains. Rating: 8

#### Intelligence
The template usefully identifies path dependence as the essential invariant: early transformations constrain downstream possibilities, while later stages may amplify, correct, or conceal earlier defects. This is a sophisticated structure rather than a simple “process has stages” observation. Rating: 8

#### Domains far apart / metanyms not synonymous
Digestion, compilation, legal procedure, film production, and metamorphic geology are exceptionally diverse in both practice and ontology. Their mappings are role-based and rarely resemble one another lexically, exceeding the Reference’s typical distinctness. Rating: 9

#### Impressive length
This is one of the Target’s more developed templates and has comparable conceptual density to several Reference templates. Nevertheless, it is still somewhat more compressed than the Reference’s long-form archetypes overall. Rating: 6

### Archetypal context 5: Renewal and Succession

#### Factually correct (per parallel context)
- PC 1 (Forest succession): This is a credible account of succession through changing light conditions, litter accumulation, shade tolerance, fire, seed banks, ash, and historical carryover. Fire does not literally initiate all seed dispersal, but the intended disturbance-mediated recruitment mechanism is sound. Rating: 8
- PC 2 (Corporate succession): The account plausibly describes organizational complexity, coordination costs, specialized entrants, bankruptcy, released assets, and inherited debt. However, bankruptcy does not necessarily expose customer relationships or enable hiring in the direct way Form (a) suggests. Rating: 7
- PC 3 (Urban redevelopment): This is a generally accurate urban-development analogy involving aging infrastructure, vacancy, demolition, inherited street patterns, reusable rubble, and phased redevelopment. Some causal claims are broad, but the Form (b) version is well calibrated. Rating: 8
- PC 4 (Software-platform succession): The software version is strong. Technical debt, maintenance burden, declining extensibility, modular replacement architectures, migration, data formats, and compatibility layers are all appropriate and factually credible. Rating: 8
- PC 5 (Ecological gut succession): The account identifies real microbiome dynamics involving substrates, metabolites, antibiotics, recolonization, resistance, and anaerobic succession. Still, biofilm is not a universal intestinal-community outcome, and antibiotics do not straightforwardly “expose microbial DNA” as the primary succession mechanism. Rating: 7

#### Beauty
Compared with the Reference, this is a particularly evocative archetype: success changes its own niche, disturbance redistributes legacy, and residue makes succession historically continuous rather than a reset. The prose maintains a satisfying ecological and temporal rhythm across domains. Rating: 8

#### Intelligence
The template insightfully distinguishes succession from replacement. Its focus on self-altering conditions, legacy effects, disturbance, and recruitment gives it more explanatory depth than a generic lifecycle metaphor. Rating: 8

#### Domains far apart / metanyms not synonymous
The contexts span forest ecology, corporate organization, urban planning, software platforms, and gut ecology. Although forest and gut cases are both ecological, the overall set retains substantial cross-domain distance and uses genuinely role-based metanyms. Rating: 8

#### Impressive length
The template is coherent and adequately detailed, but it remains concise relative to the Reference’s longer templates and therefore does not match the Reference’s 7-level impressiveness on length. Rating: 6

### Structural diversity across the submitted set
The five Target archetypes cover threshold cascades, selective boundaries, decentralized coordination, staged transformation, and succession through legacy and disturbance. This is at least as structurally varied as the Reference’s set and somewhat more cleanly separated in mechanism; however, it lacks the Reference’s explicitly demonstrated recursive hierarchy. Overall, it is clearly above the Reference benchmark but not at the maximum. Rating: 8
