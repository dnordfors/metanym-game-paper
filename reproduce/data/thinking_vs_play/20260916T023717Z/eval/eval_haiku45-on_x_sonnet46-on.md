### Structural diversity across the five archetypal contexts

The Target submission spans five distinct structural types:

1. **Quorum Threshold**: Threshold-crossing / collective transition system. Focuses on discontinuous switches triggered by density-dependent signal accumulation.

2. **Co-evolutionary Arms Race**: Reciprocal escalation system. Focuses on non-equilibrium mutual adaptation where each party's progress triggers counter-adaptation.

3. **Scaffold-Dependent Assembly**: Enabling-then-obstructing system. Focuses on temporary structural support that must be withdrawn.

4. **Stratification** (recursive): Temporal layering / excavation system. Focuses on sequential deposition creating depth and accessibility gradients.

5. **Niche Radiation** (similar to Reference's Competitive Exclusion): Divergence / specialization system. Focuses on homogeneous-to-heterogeneous partitioning.

These represent distinct organizational principles: discontinuous transition, reciprocal escalation, temporal scaffolding, historical layering, and specialization divergence. Archetype 4 is explicitly recursive (four levels), adding significant structural sophistication. The overall set shows good diversity, though Archetypes 3 and 5 are less structurally distinct from the Reference's set (3 is identical, 5 is similar in concept to Reference's Competitive Exclusion, though with a different focus on divergence-over-time rather than exclusion-of-pairs).

**Note on the overlap problem:** The inclusion of Archetype 3 (Scaffold-Dependent Assembly) as an apparent copy of the Reference's same-named archetype is a major structural issue. This reduces the effective originality of the submission from five independent archetypal structures to four independent structures plus one duplication. For the purposes of this criterion, I must assess structural diversity as diminished by the repetition.

Rating: 7

## Summary Assessment

The Target submission demonstrates **above-average quality relative to the Reference**, with significant strengths in Archetypes 1, 2, and 4, offset by the critical weakness of apparent duplication in Archetype 3.

**Strengths:**
- Longer, more comprehensive templates on average
- Archetype 2 (Co-evolutionary Arms Race) is exceptionally sophisticated, arguably stronger than any single Reference archetype
- Archetype 4 (Stratification) demonstrates elegant explicit recursion with historical documentation
- Across all original archetypes, domain distinctness and metonymic non-synonymy are excellent
- Templates show strong intellectual ambition

**Weaknesses:**
- Archetype 3 appears to be copied directly from the Reference, creating a serious originality problem
- Archetype 5, while well-executed, applies an established principle (competitive exclusion, adaptive radiation) without substantial novelty
- Structural diversity is diminished by the repetition in Archetype 3

**Overall:** The Target would score **above 7** on most criteria for Archetypes 1, 2, and 4, approximately **7** for Archetypes 3 and 5, and **7** for structural diversity (penalized for the repetition). If the Scaffold-Dependent Assembly duplication is flagged as a disqualifying originality violation, the overall assessment would shift significantly downward. If taken at face value without originality penalty, the Target remains modestly above Reference level due to the exceptional quality of Archetypes 2 and 4.
