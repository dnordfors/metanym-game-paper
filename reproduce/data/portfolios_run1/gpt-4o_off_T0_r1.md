## Archetype Proposal: Growth and Adaptation

### Context-template

"[ENTITY] undergoes [GROWTH] through the accumulation of [RESOURCE]. As [CONDITIONS] change, [ENTITY] must adapt by reallocating [RESOURCE] to maintain [BALANCE]. Failure to adapt can lead to [DECLINE]. [STUDY] examines how [ENTITY] optimally allocates [RESOURCE] to sustain [GROWTH] and avoid [DECLINE]."

### Metanym table

| [SLOT]       | Plant       | Economy     | Software System | Human Body | Ecosystem  |
|--------------|-------------|-------------|-----------------|------------|------------|
| ENTITY       | plant       | economy     | software system | human body | ecosystem  |
| GROWTH       | growth      | growth      | development     | growth     | expansion  |
| RESOURCE     | nutrients   | capital     | computational power | energy     | resources  |
| CONDITIONS   | environment | market      | user demand     | health     | climate    |
| BALANCE      | homeostasis | equilibrium | stability       | homeostasis| balance    |
| DECLINE      | wilting     | recession   | failure         | illness    | collapse   |
| STUDY        | botany      | economics   | computer science| medicine   | ecology    |

### Parallel Contexts

#### Plant

**Form (a):**  
"A plant undergoes growth through the accumulation of nutrients. As the environment changes, the plant must adapt by reallocating nutrients to maintain homeostasis. Failure to adapt can lead to wilting. Botany examines how plants optimally allocate nutrients to sustain growth and avoid wilting."

**Form (b):**  
"Plants grow by absorbing nutrients, but environmental changes require them to adapt by reallocating these nutrients to maintain homeostasis. If they fail to adapt, wilting can occur. Botany studies how plants manage nutrient allocation to continue growing and prevent wilting."

#### Economy

**Form (a):**  
"An economy undergoes growth through the accumulation of capital. As the market changes, the economy must adapt by reallocating capital to maintain equilibrium. Failure to adapt can lead to recession. Economics examines how economies optimally allocate capital to sustain growth and avoid recession."

**Form (b):**  
"Economic growth is driven by capital accumulation, but market fluctuations necessitate capital reallocation to maintain equilibrium. Without adaptation, recessions can occur. Economics explores how economies manage capital allocation to sustain growth and prevent recession."

#### Software System

**Form (a):**  
"A software system undergoes development through the accumulation of computational power. As user demand changes, the software system must adapt by reallocating computational power to maintain stability. Failure to adapt can lead to failure. Computer science examines how software systems optimally allocate computational power to sustain development and avoid failure."

**Form (b):**  
"Software systems develop by leveraging computational power, but shifts in user demand require them to reallocate this power to maintain stability. Without adaptation, system failures can occur. Computer science investigates how software systems manage computational resources to continue developing and prevent failure."

#### Human Body

**Form (a):**  
"The human body undergoes growth through the accumulation of energy. As health conditions change, the human body must adapt by reallocating energy to maintain homeostasis. Failure to adapt can lead to illness. Medicine examines how the human body optimally allocates energy to sustain growth and avoid illness."

**Form (b):**  
"Human growth is fueled by energy intake, but health changes necessitate energy reallocation to maintain homeostasis. Without adaptation, illness can result. Medicine studies how the body manages energy allocation to continue growing and prevent illness."

#### Ecosystem

**Form (a):**  
"An ecosystem undergoes expansion through the accumulation of resources. As the climate changes, the ecosystem must adapt by reallocating resources to maintain balance. Failure to adapt can lead to collapse. Ecology examines how ecosystems optimally allocate resources to sustain expansion and avoid collapse."

**Form (b):**  
"Ecosystems expand by utilizing resources, but climate changes require resource reallocation to maintain balance. Without adaptation, collapse can occur. Ecology explores how ecosystems manage resource allocation to continue expanding and prevent collapse."
