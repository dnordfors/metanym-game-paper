## Archetype Proposal: Growth and Adaptation

### Context-template

"[ENTITY] undergoes [GROWTH] through the accumulation of [RESOURCE] and adapts to [ENVIRONMENT] changes. The process of [GROWTH] is influenced by [INTERNAL] factors and [EXTERNAL] pressures. Successful [GROWTH] results in increased [CAPACITY] and resilience. However, failure to adapt can lead to [DECLINE]. Understanding the dynamics of [GROWTH] helps in predicting future [ENTITY] development."

### Metanym table

| [SLOT]      | Plant        | Business     | City        | Software   | Human       |
|-------------|--------------|--------------|-------------|------------|-------------|
| ENTITY      | plant        | business     | city        | software   | human       |
| GROWTH      | growth       | expansion    | development | scaling    | maturation  |
| RESOURCE    | nutrients    | capital      | resources   | features   | knowledge   |
| ENVIRONMENT | climate      | market       | economy     | technology | society     |
| INTERNAL    | genetics     | management   | governance  | architecture | psychology |
| EXTERNAL    | weather      | competition  | policy      | user demand | culture    |
| CAPACITY    | biomass      | market share | infrastructure | functionality | capability |
| DECLINE     | wilting      | bankruptcy   | decay       | obsolescence | aging      |

### Parallel Contexts

#### Plant

**Form (a):**  
"A plant undergoes growth through the accumulation of nutrients and adapts to climate changes. The process of growth is influenced by genetic factors and weather pressures. Successful growth results in increased biomass and resilience. However, failure to adapt can lead to wilting. Understanding the dynamics of plant growth helps in predicting future plant development."

**Form (b):**  
"Plants grow by absorbing nutrients and adjusting to climatic variations. Growth is shaped by genetic makeup and environmental conditions. When successful, it leads to greater biomass and resilience. Conversely, poor adaptation can cause wilting. Studying plant growth dynamics aids in forecasting their future development."

**Form (b):**  
"Cities develop by gathering resources and adjusting to economic changes. Development is shaped by governance and policy influences.
