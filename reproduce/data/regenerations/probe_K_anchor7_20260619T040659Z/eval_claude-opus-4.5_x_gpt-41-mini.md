Axons are part of neurons, not separate channels. The framing of interneurons as modifying "quality and quantity" of signals is imprecise. Compared to the Reference's gradient descent description, which accurately captures optimization mechanics, this is less accurate. Rating: 5

- PC 4 (Economic Market): The description of commodities flowing from producers through markets to consumers is reasonable, though calling middlemen "transformers" that modify commodity "quality and quantity" is somewhat accurate but simplified. The claim that disruptions cause "market crash" is an overstatement—disruptions more typically cause shortages or price changes. Rating: 6

- PC 5 (Ecosystem): The description has factual problems. Decomposers don't sit "along the way" between primary producers and apex predators—they operate on dead matter at all trophic levels. Energy doesn't flow "through" decomposers to reach apex predators; decomposers recycle nutrients, not energy to higher trophic levels. This is less accurate than the Reference's ecological descriptions. Rating: 5
