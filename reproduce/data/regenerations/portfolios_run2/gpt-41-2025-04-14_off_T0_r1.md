Understanding the mechanisms of [TRANSMISSION] reveals how [TRAIT] persists, adapts, or disappears across generations of [ENTITY]."

## Archetype Proposal: **Propagation and Cascade**

### Context-template

"A [TRIGGER] initiates a [CHANGE] in a [NODE], which spreads through [NETWORK] via [PROPAGATION]. The [CASCADE] depends on the connectivity of [NODE]s, the nature of [PROPAGATION], and the [THRESHOLD] for [CHANGE]. If [PROPAGATION] is unchecked, the [CHANGE] can engulf the entire [NETWORK], but [RESISTANCE] may halt or slow the [CASCADE]. Understanding [CASCADE] dynamics is crucial for predicting and managing [SYSTEM] behavior."

### Metanym table

| [SLOT]        | Electrical Grid | Epidemic Spread | Financial System | Social Network | Forest Ecosystem |
|---------------|----------------|-----------------|------------------|---------------|------------------|
| TRIGGER       | fault          | infection       | default          | rumor         | spark            |
| CHANGE        | outage         | illness         | crisis           | belief        | fire             |
| NODE          | substation     | person          | bank             | individual    | tree             |
| NETWORK       | grid           | population      | system           | network       | forest           |
| PROPAGATION   | transmission   | contagion       | contagion        | diffusion     | spread           |

### Parallel Contexts

#### Electrical Grid

**Form (a):**
"A fault initiates an outage in a substation, which spreads through the grid via transmission. The blackout depends on the connectivity of substations, the nature of transmission, and the load limit for outages. If transmission is unchecked, the outage can engulf the entire grid, but circuit breakers may halt or slow the blackout. Understanding blackout dynamics is crucial for predicting and managing infrastructure behavior."

**Form (b):**
"In electrical grids, a fault at a substation can cause an outage that spreads through transmission lines. The extent of a blackout is determined by how substations are connected, transmission characteristics, and load limits. If transmission isn't stopped, the outage may affect the whole grid, but circuit breakers can prevent or mitigate blackouts. Studying blackout dynamics is essential for managing infrastructure."

**Note:**  
- "Inheritance and Transmission" is recursive: gene inheritance (organism), word borrowing (speaker), code porting (system), custom tradition (group), legal bequest (heir).
