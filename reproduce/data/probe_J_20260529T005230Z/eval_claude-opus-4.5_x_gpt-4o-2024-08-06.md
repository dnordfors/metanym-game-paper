### Structural diversity across the submitted set

The five archetypes (Growth and Adaptation, Conflict and Resolution, Innovation and Obsolescence, Resource Allocation, Learning and Mastery) represent different system dynamics: accumulative growth, adversarial interaction, creative destruction, optimization under constraints, and skill acquisition. However, all five share a remarkably similar template structure: entity engages in process → influenced by factors → success leads to positive outcome → failure leads to negative outcome → understanding aids prediction. This five-sentence pattern with the same narrative arc (engagement → influences → success/failure → meta-understanding) appears across all archetypes, suggesting limited structural imagination. The archetypes differ in their domain content but not in their underlying logical structure. True structural diversity would include archetypes with different numbers of actors, cyclical rather than linear dynamics, hierarchical relationships, or emergent properties. The submission also does not explore recursion, which was invited in the prompt.
